# Risk Assessment & Risk Register

## Overview
This activity, completed as part of the Google Cybersecurity Certificate, focuses on assessing and prioritizing cybersecurity and operational risks in a fictional banking environment.

## Objectives
- Identify potential risks to organizational assets and sensitive information.
- Evaluate each risk based on **Likelihood** and **Severity** (1–3).
- Calculate risk priority scores using **Likelihood × Impact**.
- Prioritize risks based on their potential business impact.

## Key Findings
The assessment identified **financial records exposure** as the highest-priority risk (9/9), followed by a compromised user database (6/9).

## Skills Demonstrated
- Risk Assessment
- Risk Register Documentation
- Risk Scoring and Prioritization
- Cybersecurity Risk Management
